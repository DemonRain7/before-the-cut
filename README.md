# Before the Cut

## Current counter rules

The student selected wear and the warned second PRESS on November 21. Half of steady visitors can show one worn row. The protected opening visitor has none. Two broken rows establish drift. The second PRESS needs two lamps, takes all lamps and pays 150 at closing for a successful shutter catch. Reopened tools and correct codes are free and safe. CALM and actions that take a lamp can trigger surprises. Closing reveals the diagnosis. These rules have been measured by simulation and await outside players.


A Godot 4 game about examining patients before cybernetic surgery. You compare a patient's appearance and responses with a reference sheet of the normal states of the implants that patient wears, choose examinations or dialogue in any order, and decide whether to approve surgery or call in Trauma Team. Every examination is optional. Defend yourself if threatened. Fired by a megacorp and deep in debt, the doctor works the counter of a street gang's back-street clinic, where two of each night's visitors are contacts to be picked out from tonight's list and given the right code. The game is in English or Chinese (中文); the switch is on the title card.

This is one student's ongoing course project. The fictional cyberpsychosis premise is inspired by Cyberpunk 2077; the patients and screening rules are original game design.

## Run it

Windows, PowerShell, from the repository root. The launcher looks for Godot 4.7.2 in `$env:GODOT_BIN`, then `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CodexTools\Godot\4.7.2\`, then on `PATH` (or pass `-GodotPath <exe>`). In a fresh clone its first run imports the pictures, fonts and sounds before the game starts (a few seconds; `-ImportOnly` does only that).

```powershell
.\run-game.ps1                 # saved settings, initially the scripted patient
.\run-game.ps1 -LessBlood      # the attack scenes without blood
```

The title menu has START, HOW TO PLAY, SETTINGS and QUIT. SETTINGS keeps the language (English or 中文), blood, sound, screen mode and patient AI choice.

The launch commands below take you directly to reproducible demonstration nights. The scripted and mock commands are free. The live command starts the game's local HTTP service, then calls OpenAI GPT-6 Luna after you enter `LIVE`. In SETTINGS, enabling PATIENT AI with no launch flags connects to the public mock.

```powershell
.\run-game.ps1 -Patient script -Language zh -TestNight I -Seed 41307
.\run-game.ps1 -Patient mock -Language en -TestNight I -Seed 41307
.\run-game.ps1 -Patient live -Language zh -TestNight J -Seed 41307
.\run-game.ps1 -Patient timeout -Language en
```

Controls and rules are in [game/README.md](game/README.md); providers, keys and limits in [agent_proxy/README.md](agent_proxy/README.md). The source was moved here from the earlier local workspace on November 15, 2026 ([transition record](docs/development/repository-transition.md)).

## Design and verification

The [agent manifest](agent-config.json) and [production-agent artifacts](agent_team/) describe the bounded patient agent and planned development roles. The patient AI writes dialogue and reactions from the case, the conversation and the signs the player has uncovered. Typed and confirmed spoken questions follow the ASK menu rules. ASK uses the authored answer by default, and the full game is playable offline.

On November 21, 2026, the game's service sent 1,094 real GPT-6 Luna evaluation requests across English and Chinese. Each appointment starts a fresh AI session. The patient can refer back to what the doctor said, whether the grip gauge was used and how often the doctor applied pressure. A semantic router recognizes case questions, follow-ups, tool requests, reassurance, provocations, social or personal talk, role challenges and ambiguous or other input. A paid inferred question shows its price for confirmation. The patient can volunteer a short line on arrival, after a check or in a lull. The evaluation retained authored answers for graded ASK. The three permanent Workers passed deployment checks and live stays disabled. Nobody has played these rules. [Appointment memory and cache checks](HANDOFF.md#one-session-per-appointment) · [Five-minute demonstration](HANDOFF.md#five-minutes-at-the-counter)

On November 15, 2026, the first live OpenAI run completed 30 patient requests and ten transcriptions of local synthetic speech, all successful. Twelve dialogue requests were played by an agent in the real window; eighteen were HTTP probes. No human microphone, listening or playtest result is claimed. A feedback bug found during the run was fixed: generated speech now retains what the patient actually said. See the [live report](docs/development/live-model-results-2026-11-15.md).

On November 16 the student's eleven notes on that build were answered in the game: a patient whose patience is gone leaves by themselves; the till is read every hour against a line set by the doctor's lender; the shutter is a timing bar, and the counter glass wears down with every blow that lands; the intake card counts the player's own crosses against the sheet's house rule; there is a short opening, a counter guide, and a thinking pose while the patient AI answers. These are assistant proposals with untuned numbers.

Later the same day the student asked for a more immersive screen (keep only the counter, with the visitor across it on one large view, and turn everything else into things on the desk or the walls that open when clicked) and for a way to pay for repairs to the counter glass that earlier attackers damaged. The two requests are the student's. The layout, the keys, the price and the wording are assistant proposals.

On November 17 the student sent five notes on that build: pass and fail were unclear, since nothing pointed at the reference sheet (how long a grip is reasonable, for example); CALM should go or cost money; the grip and the eye test were confusing, and the opening should walk the player through everything once; a patient should be able to fly into a rage in the middle of any action; and a new story, in which the doctor, fired by a megacorp and in debt, works for a gang at a street implant clinic, contacts come in to exchange codes and smuggle military implants, a daily contact list is used to cross-reference the visitors, and a wrong code costs patience.
