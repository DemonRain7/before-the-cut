## Agreed design

- Godot 4, 2D pixel art, a clinic counter. The player is an implant doctor examining patients before surgery.
- A Normal Reference Sheet shows expected appearance, states and responses. Players compare it with the patient and make their own judgments.
- Observation, tools, ordinary dialogue and provocative verbal pressure are optional and unordered. Players may skip any method and decide early. Do not reinstate a mandatory checklist, universal re-check or fixed examination sequence.
- The player either approves surgery (TREAT) or calls Trauma Team to take the patient; a patient who turns is stopped with the shutter.
- Patient backgrounds provide continuity. The AI generates original dialogue and reactions from the character and conversation. The game settles tool guarantees and whether a question is answered or dodged. ASK uses case-authored words by default. A first unsettled instrument request can be refused. The service validates the stated outcome, format and length. Refusal or irritation alone is not a diagnosis. The game owns case truth, readings, attacks and scoring.
- Every generated turn uses the same patient's own identity, work, voice and motive, plus the current appointment's ordered dialogue, reactions and performed actions. Opening the chair starts a fresh session even for a returning patient. The ten-class router handles a case question, a continued conversation or other intent in either language. Only an explicit PRESS performs pressure. A paid inferred question displays the topic and lamp price for confirmation. Only earned SPEECH and TEMPER cues enter the dialogue prompt.
- Optional lines on arrival, after a check and after 12 quiet seconds can use three of the 16 replies per visit, including at most two idle turns. Player input cancels an outstanding optional line. The public menu endpoint is the permanent mock. The designated live launcher uses OpenAI GPT-6 Luna.
- Keep the stateful scripted patient as the offline comparison and fallback. The November 21 model evaluation left graded ASK authored. Whether generated dialogue improves the game awaits the outside-player comparison.
- Fantasy and Discovery are the primary motivations; Sensation supports the suspense.
- The student wants the agents to develop art, presentation and implementation creatively. Exact signs, cases, balance and patient counts are proposals, not frozen requirements. The cap of 16 patient replies per visit stays (student, November 15).

## Languages

The player picks English or Chinese (中文) on the title card. Every word the game shows is written in English, in `game/data` and the scripts; the Chinese is a set of catalogs in `game/data/lang/zh/`, keyed by that exact English (`game/scripts/core/lang.gd`). A change on one side is a change on both (student, November 18):

- Changing or adding shown English means writing its Chinese line in the same change, and changing a translation means checking that it still says what the English says. Names and code names stay English; companies, gangs and organizations are translated. The glossary and the patients' voices are in `docs/development/chinese-translation.md`.
- Words painted into pictures live in `tools/art/words.py`. Change them there and run the generator, which writes the English picture and its twin `<name>_zh.png` together.
- Before committing, run `python tools/check_lang.py` (a missing, stale or mismatched line, or a missing picture twin), `python measure_text.py` and `python measure_text.py --lang zh` in `tools/art` (every line fits in both languages), and the UI smoke test in both languages (`... --script res://tests/run_ui_smoke.gd -- --lang=zh` fails on any English that reached the screen untranslated).
- The patient model answers in the player's language (the proxy's `language` field); the scripted patient's lines and key words are in the case catalogs and `lang/zh/topics.json`.
