# Patient character prompt

Each case's `intake` contains the printed entry, a separate attachment and what this person first remembers. For Rook the location on his repair form differs from the site of the jam. Both locations appear on the tow slip. His first account is constant. When the doctor asks how it fits, Godot selects the explanation for this appointment's SPEECH sign and keeps the exact words in the history and on the file. A focused PRESS supplies an independent TEMPER cue and can generate a short reaction in the patient's voice. Later model turns receive only the file material that has come up. The patient opens a clean file at each arrival. [Rook's case](../game/data/cases/rook.json) · [Visit state](../game/scripts/core/visit_state.gd)

All ten patients share the same performance and evidence instructions. Each case has one authored `soul` for the person's voice, motives and backstory, alongside their name, work and reason for visiting. To change Rook's opinion of his foreman, edit Rook's case. To change how every patient handles an ambiguous follow-up, edit the shared instructions. The dialogue builder places these assets before the current appointment. [Shared instructions](../agent_proxy/src/patient_instructions.mjs) · [Rook's case](../game/data/cases/rook.json) · [Prompt assembly](../agent_proxy/src/core.mjs)

The session history exists in Godot for the current appointment. A visit can contain dialogue, visible reactions and physical actions. Closing discards the full timeline and the bounded cache of successful intent classifications. Character assets are read-only at runtime. A returning patient receives the same soul and a new visit history. [Session lifecycle](../game/scripts/core/patient_agent.gd) · [History sent to the model](../game/scripts/core/visit_record.gd)

The doctor can type freely or review and send a voice transcript. The intent router selects a case question or a conversational class, including personal talk, follow-up, role challenges and ambiguity. A listed question receives its free answer immediately. A complaint question can direct the player to a test. Godot keeps one ordered record for each appointment. It contains what the doctor said, the replies and reactions shown, and committed actions such as opening GRIP, finishing it and applying PRESS. The fixed character and behavior instructions precede this record. The current event, available cues and promised outcome follow it. Excerpts used for very long appointments include action totals. Each arrival opens a fresh session.

Return the patient's own short `reply` and visible `reaction`. A tool request or a named outcome also needs `cooperates`. Godot grants the instrument and owns every reading and lamp. A complaint event supplies the rows that can be voiced now and only earlier complaints from this visit. The exact authored sentence is available if a part is missing from the reply, a test result is asserted, or a request fails. SPEECH and TEMPER cues enter only after their evidence is earned. A role challenge is answered as the same patient. The authored ASK line supplies the scored evidence in both modes.

The authored opening plays before an optional model line. The patient may speak on arrival, after an examination, or after 12 quiet seconds. Silence on those turns is valid. Three optional attempts, including at most two idle attempts, share the 16 dialogue replies per visit. The game discards late optional answers when the player acts. Foreground transport and validation failures use the labelled authored fallback.
